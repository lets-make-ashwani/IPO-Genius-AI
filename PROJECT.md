# IPO Genius AI

## Project Description

IPO Genius AI is an AI-powered SaaS platform that helps users research, analyze, and understand upcoming IPOs through AI-generated insights, financial analysis, and automated workflows.

The goal is to simplify IPO research by combining public IPO information, AI analysis, and automation into one platform.

---

# Project Goal

Build a modern, scalable, and production-ready IPO research platform.

The application should be:

- Fast
- Responsive
- AI Powered
- Easy to Maintain
- Modular
- Production Ready
