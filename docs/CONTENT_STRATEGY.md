# IPO Genius AI — Content Strategy & AIEO Blueprint

* **Version:** `1.0.1` (Phase 5.3)

---

## 1. IPO Detail Page Structure

Every IPO detail page follows a structured content hierarchy:
1. **Hero Header:** Company name, status badge, sector, BSE/NSE exchange tags, AI rating score.
2. **KPI Highlights:** Price band range, GMP premium percentage, issue size, listing date.
3. **Company Overview:** Core business model, promoter background, lead managers, registrar.
4. **Financial Summary:** Revenue, PAT margins, EBITDA margins, valuation multiples.
5. **SWOT Analysis:** Strengths, Weaknesses, Growth Opportunities, Regulatory Risk Factors.
6. **GEO Verdict:** Explicit "Recommended For" and "Who Should Avoid" investor guidance.

---

## 2. Content Freshness Policy

* All AI evaluation scores display a real-time timestamp indicator (`Updated: HH:MM IST`).
* Scraped exchange data (NSE, BSE, GMP) revalidates dynamically.
