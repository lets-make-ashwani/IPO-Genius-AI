# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Project Name

IPO Genius AI

---

# Product Overview

IPO Genius AI is an AI-powered web platform that helps users research and understand upcoming IPOs through AI-generated analysis, financial insights, risk assessment, and automation.

The platform combines IPO information, AI analysis, notifications, and educational content into one modern SaaS application.

---

# Problem Statement

Currently, IPO information is scattered across multiple websites.

Users have to:

- Visit multiple websites
- Read lengthy DRHP documents
- Watch YouTube videos
- Compare IPOs manually
- Search for financial information

IPO Genius AI solves this by bringing everything together in one platform.

# Success Criteria

The MVP is successful if users can:

- Create an account
- Explore IPOs
- Read AI analysis
- Save IPOs
- Receive notifications
- Purchase a subscription
- Use the platform on desktop and mobile

---

# Non-Goals (MVP)

The MVP will NOT include:

- Mobile Apps
- Live Trading
- Stock Brokerage
- Portfolio Management
- Mutual Funds
- Cryptocurrency
- Social Network

These may be added in future versions.
