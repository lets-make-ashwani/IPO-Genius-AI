# IPO Genius AI - Brand Guidelines

# Brand Name

IPO Genius AI

---

# Brand Mission

Help every investor understand IPOs through Artificial Intelligence.

Provide clear, simple, and trustworthy IPO analysis.

---

# Brand Personality

The brand should feel

- Professional
- Intelligent
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Premium
- Clean
- Innovative
- Data Driven

Avoid

- Cartoon style
- Playful design
- Overly colorful UI
- Gaming style
- Crypto style

---

- Retail Investors
- Beginners
- Traders
- Long-Term Investors
- Students
- Financial Professionals

- Stripe
